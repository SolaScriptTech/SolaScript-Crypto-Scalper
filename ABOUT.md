# About

SolaScript Crypto Scalper is a sequence of Python trading experiments by Matthew Johns focused on short-horizon cryptocurrency signals and automated position management.

The project moved into its own repository because the SolaScript versions form a recognizable, independent lineage rather than unrelated one-off bots. The archive preserves that evolution while making v5.2 the clearly identified current reference.

Publication is for education and research. It does not establish profitability, operational safety, or suitability for any trader.
